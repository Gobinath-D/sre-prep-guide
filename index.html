How to use this book

  • Read a chapter top to bottom once. It starts gentle, so don't skip the analogy — it's the hook the internals hang on.
  • Actually run the labs. Reading about a TCP handshake and watching one in tcpdump are different skills, and only one gets you hired.
  • Hit a Think first box? Stop and answer it in your head before you click. That reflex is the whole point.
  • Work in order — chapters lean on earlier ones — but the distributed-systems track (Part IX) is a fine detour any time after networking.
